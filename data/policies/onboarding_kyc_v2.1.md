# Customer Onboarding and KYC Policy (v2.1)

## 1. Purpose and scope
This policy sets the minimum know-your-customer (KYC) checks Fernleaf Payments Ltd performs before opening a personal or business e-money account. It applies to every onboarding channel, including the mobile app and partner referrals. Version 2.1 lowers the proof-of-address and EDD thresholds following the 2023 internal audit.

## 2. Identity verification
Every customer must pass identity verification before the account is activated. Accepted identity documents are a valid passport, a national identity card or a driving licence with photo. The document check must be paired with a liveness check. Accounts may not receive or send funds until identity verification is complete.

## 3. Proof of address
Proof of address is required when a customer's expected monthly transaction volume is above $5,000. Below $5,000 per month, an electronic address check against a credit reference agency is sufficient and no document is required. Accepted proof-of-address documents are a utility bill, a bank statement or a government tax letter, each dated within the last 3 months. Mobile phone bills are not accepted.

## 4. Enhanced due diligence
Enhanced due diligence (EDD) is required when the customer is a politically exposed person (PEP), is resident in a high-risk jurisdiction, or expects monthly volume above $25,000. EDD includes a source-of-funds declaration, a source-of-wealth check for PEPs and senior management approval before the account is activated.

## 5. Periodic review
High-risk customers are re-verified every 24 months. Standard-risk customers are re-verified every 60 months or when their details change, whichever comes first.
