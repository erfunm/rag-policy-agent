# Vendor Risk Management Policy (v1.0)

## 1. Vendor tiers
Vendors are tiered as critical, important or standard. A vendor is critical if its failure would stop customer payments or it processes customer personal data at scale.

## 2. Due diligence
Critical vendors must provide an independent SOC 2 Type II report or ISO 27001 certificate before contract signature, and annually after that. A data processing agreement is required for any vendor that processes personal data.

## 3. Exit plans
Every critical vendor must have a documented and tested exit plan. Exit plans are tested every 2 years.

## 4. Ongoing monitoring
Critical vendors are reviewed every year, important vendors every 2 years and standard vendors every 3 years.
