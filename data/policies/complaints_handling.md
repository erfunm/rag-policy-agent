# Complaints Handling Policy (v2.2)

## 1. What counts as a complaint
Any expression of dissatisfaction, oral or written, about our service is a complaint, whether or not the customer uses the word "complaint". Complaints must be logged in the complaints system on the day they are received.

## 2. Timelines
Complaints are acknowledged within 2 business days. A final response for complaints about payment services must be sent within 15 business days. In exceptional circumstances a holding response may be sent, and the final response must then follow within 35 business days.

## 3. Final response
The final response must explain the outcome, any redress offered, and the customer's right to refer the complaint to the Financial Ombudsman within 6 months.

## 4. Root-cause analysis
The Customer Operations team reviews complaint themes every month and reports them to the Risk Committee every quarter.
