# Incident Response Policy (v1.2)

## 1. Severity levels
SEV1 means customer payments are unavailable or data is confirmed exposed. SEV2 means a major feature is degraded. SEV3 means a minor issue with a workaround.

## 2. Roles
For every SEV1 incident an incident commander must be appointed within 15 minutes of declaration. The incident commander owns communication and decides when the incident is resolved.

## 3. Regulatory notification
A major operational incident must be reported to the financial regulator within 4 hours of being classified as major. A personal data breach that is likely to put people at risk must be reported to the data protection authority within 72 hours of the company becoming aware of it.

## 4. Post-incident review
A blameless post-incident review is held within 10 business days of every SEV1 or SEV2 incident.
