# Data Retention Standard (v1.4)

## 1. Principles
Personal data is kept only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected for, or as long as law or regulation requires. When the retention period ends, records are deleted or irreversibly anonymised within 90 days.

## 2. Retention periods
KYC records, including identity documents and proof of address, are retained for 5 years after the customer relationship ends. Transaction records are retained for 5 years from the date of the transaction. System audit logs are retained for 7 years. Call recordings with customers are retained for 12 months unless linked to an open complaint or investigation.

## 3. Marketing preferences
Records of marketing consent are deleted 24 months after the customer withdraws consent or closes the account.

## 4. Legal holds
A legal hold issued by the Legal team suspends deletion for the records it covers until the hold is lifted.
