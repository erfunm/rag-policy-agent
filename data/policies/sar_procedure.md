# Suspicious Activity Reporting Procedure (v1.0)

## 1. Restricted procedure
This procedure is restricted to the Compliance function because disclosing it could amount to tipping off.

## 2. MLRO decision
The MLRO must decide whether to file a suspicious activity report within 5 business days of receiving an internal suspicion report. The decision and its reasoning are recorded in the SAR register.

## 3. Filing
If the MLRO decides to file, the SAR is submitted to the national Financial Intelligence Unit through its online portal within 24 hours of the decision. Where a defence against money laundering is needed, the related payment is held for up to 7 business days awaiting consent.

## 4. Tipping off
Nobody may tell the customer, or anyone outside Compliance, that a SAR has been considered or filed.
