# Fraud Escalation Procedure (v1.1)

## 1. Suspected account takeover
If an agent suspects account takeover, for example after a password reset from a new device followed by a payment to a new payee, the account must be frozen within 15 minutes and the case escalated to Fraud Operations through the fraud queue.

## 2. Authorised push payment scams
When a customer reports that they were tricked into sending a payment, the agent records the scam report and sends a recall request to the receiving bank the same business day. A reimbursement decision must be made within 5 business days of the report.

## 3. Card fraud
Disputes about card transactions that the customer did not authorise are handled under the Card Disputes Procedure (POL-CRD-003) and coded as fraud disputes.

## 4. Customer contact
Agents must never ask a customer for their full card number, PIN or one-time passcode, including during a fraud investigation.
