# AML Transaction Monitoring Standard (v3.0)

## 1. Scope
All customer transactions are screened by the automated monitoring system after settlement. This standard defines alert handling for the Financial Crime Operations team.

## 2. Monitoring scenarios
The system raises alerts for: structuring, meaning three or more deposits between $9,000 and $9,999 within 7 days; rapid movement of funds, meaning more than 80% of incoming funds sent out within 48 hours; and payments to or from high-risk jurisdictions above $2,000.

## 3. Alert triage
Level 1 analysts must triage every alert within 2 business days of it being raised. An alert can be closed only with a written rationale. Alerts that cannot be explained by the customer's profile are escalated to Level 2 investigators.

## 4. Investigation and escalation
Level 2 investigations must be completed within 30 calendar days. If an investigator suspects money laundering, the case is escalated to the MLRO through an internal suspicion report. Analysts must not discuss the suspicion with the customer.
