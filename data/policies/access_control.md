# Access Control Policy (v2.0)

## 1. Least privilege
Staff receive only the access their role needs. Access is requested through the identity portal and approved by the line manager and the system owner.

## 2. Authentication
Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all staff accounts and all remote access. Shared accounts are prohibited.

## 3. Leavers and movers
Access for leavers is removed within 24 hours of their last working day. When staff move roles, access that the new role does not need is removed within 5 business days.

## 4. Access reviews
System owners review user access every quarter. Privileged access is reviewed every month, and production break-glass access requires approval from the on-call engineering manager.
