# Card Disputes and Chargebacks Procedure (v1.6)

## 1. Raising a dispute
Customers must raise a card dispute within 120 days of the transaction date. Disputes raised later are declined unless the card scheme rules allow an exception.

## 2. Provisional credit
For disputes over $25, the customer receives a provisional credit within 10 business days while the chargeback is pursued. Provisional credit is reversed if the merchant successfully defends the chargeback.

## 3. Fraud-coded disputes
Disputes where the customer did not authorise the transaction are coded as fraud. For fraud-coded disputes the card is cancelled and replaced immediately and the case is also referred to Fraud Operations under the Fraud Escalation Procedure (POL-FRD-012).

## 4. Chargeback submission
Card Operations submits the chargeback to the card scheme within 5 business days of receiving all evidence.
